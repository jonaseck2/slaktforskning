Bottom line

The sweet spot is hybrid: use Cytoscape for the network graph (C) where it's the right tool, and custom SVG for pedigree (A), hourglass (B), and timeline (D) where the layouts are deterministic and Cytoscape fights you. The bundle cost (~300KB) is justified by a single view that would otherwise take weeks to hand-roll. All four views live as separate Vue components — the SVG ones never touch Cytoscape.

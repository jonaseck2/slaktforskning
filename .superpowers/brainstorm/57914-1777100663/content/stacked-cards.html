What "pull to open" means
The card header matches the sub-panel header exactly — same badge, same colours. Clicking a row or the header opens the sub-panel to the right. The visual language says "this card can be pulled out".
Dropdowns — text only
No icons in search results. Just clean text rows with a subtle meta label on the right (e.g. "link" for existing, nothing for create). Keeps scanning fast.

Each sub-panel is its own rounded card — they grow to the right

Event → Place → Parent Place. Each level is a distinct card with its own × in the top-right. Closing level 2 returns to level 1 active. Closing level 1 returns to level 0 active. Panels slightly shrink at depth to give a perspective cue. Color-coded headers make depth readable.

Depth limit consideration: 3 levels (main + 2 sub-panels) is the practical max before it gets unwieldy. At depth ≥ 3, the innermost panel's "create" option could fall back to select-only, or the panels could scroll horizontally within a capped container width.

Load-more vs. collapse — same or different button style?

At the deepest loaded generation, a person may have ancestors in the DB that haven't been fetched yet. The button that loads them could look like the existing collapse buttons, or be visually distinct.

A

Distinct: dashed border + "+" label

Load-more buttons use a dashed circle outline and a "+" symbol. Once you click and the generation loads, the button becomes the standard solid-circle collapse toggle. Clear difference between "fetch new data" and "show/hide loaded data".

+
load more
▶
collapse
B

Same style — directional arrow

Load-more and collapse buttons look identical. Both are solid circles with the directional arrow. Less visual noise; the action is always "toggle/load in this direction".

▶
load more / collapse — same button

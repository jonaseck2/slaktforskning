How should "expand more" work at the edge of the tree?

When you reach a leaf node (deepest loaded ancestor or descendant), how many generations should clicking the expand button load?

A

One generation at a time

Each click loads exactly the next generation. Keep clicking to go deeper. Simple and predictable — never loads more than you asked for.

B

Same chunk as initial load

Pedigree loads 4 generations at a time; hourglass loads 3 ancestors + 3 descendants. Expanding at the edge loads the same-sized chunk deeper. Fewer clicks needed.

C

All available ancestors / descendants

When you expand a leaf node, load everything in the database from that node outward. No further clicking needed — but could be a very large tree.

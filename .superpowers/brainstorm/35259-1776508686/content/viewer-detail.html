Interaction Details

Image viewer

  • Scroll to zoom, drag to pan
  • Arrow keys or nav buttons to prev/next
  • Zoom controls in toolbar (−, +, Fit)
  • Face tag regions shown as dashed overlays
  • Hover a face tag → highlight in panel

Face tagging workflow

  • "+ Draw" in panel enters draw mode
  • Click-drag on image to draw rectangle
  • PersonPicker popup to assign person
  • Unassigned regions shown as "?" (amber)
  • Click region in panel → highlights on image

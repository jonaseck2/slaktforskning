A. Warm Heritage

Earthy tones, warm browns, cream backgrounds. Evokes old documents, archives, family history. Approachable and calm. Sidebar is dark walnut. Initials avatars. Reduced sidebar to 7 items with progressive disclosure.

B. Clean Scandinavian

Light, airy, precise. Inspired by Linear/Notion. White sidebar with subtle borders, blue accents, excellent contrast. Feels modern, professional, tech-forward. Squared avatars. Keyboard shortcut hints visible.

C. Soft & Personal

Purple/lavender tones, soft gradients, generous rounding. Feels personal and inviting — like a family journal, not a database. Dark purple sidebar. Circular avatars with gender gradients. Slightly playful without being unserious.

What all three share

  • Reduced sidebar — 7 items instead of 14, advanced features behind "More" or settings
  • Initials/avatar on person rows — visual identity instead of just text
  • Consistent component library — buttons, badges, inputs, modals all from one design token set
  • Birth info shown inline — most useful context visible without clicking in
  • Keyboard shortcut hints — discoverable power-user features

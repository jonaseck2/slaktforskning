# Examples

## `swedish-royals.db`

A populated demo database — the Swedish royal **House of Holstein-Gottorp** (1751–1818), which ruled Sweden in the dynasty immediately before Bernadotte. Used for every screenshot in [README.md](../README.md), [QUICKSTART.md](../QUICKSTART.md), and [MANUAL.md](../MANUAL.md).

Open it from the running app via **Settings → Database → Open** and pick this file. Your other databases stay intact; you can switch back at any time.

### What's in it

- **10 persons**: Adolf Fredrik, Lovisa Ulrika of Prussia, their children Gustav III / Karl XIII / Fredrik Adolf / Sofia Albertina, three queens consort, and Gustav IV Adolf (the last king of the dynasty, deposed in the 1809 coup).
- **4 marriages**, 10 parent-child links.
- **11 events** with structured dates — births, deaths (with `cause` on the dramatic ones — the assassination of Gustav III, the stroke that killed Gustav IV Adolf in exile).
- **8 places** spanning Sweden, Denmark, Germany, France, Switzerland.
- **11 portraits** in `swedish-royals-media/` — Wikimedia Commons sourced, all `PD-old-100` (painters died > 100 years ago). Credits in [docs/manual/image-credits.md](../docs/manual/image-credits.md).
- **3 face-tagged regions** on Alexander Roslin's 1771 group portrait *"Gustav III and his brothers"* — the showcase image for the face-tag feature.
- **1 source** (Svenskt biografiskt lexikon) linked to **1 repository** (Riksarkivet).
- **1 group** ("Holstein-Gottorpska kungahuset") with three members.
- **1 open research task**.

All persons died ≥ 1829; no living-person concerns.
